| Přichycení na křivku | `addConstraint` (`pointOnCurve`) ve stejném `batch` | |

Typ kóty podle výběru:

| Výběr | Vazba |
|---|---|
| dva body | `distance`, při vodorovném nebo svislém umístění `horizontalDistance` / `verticalDistance` |
| úsečka | `distance` mezi jejími koncovými body (not an axis of the origin: it has no length, D71) |
| bod a úsečka | `distancePointLine` |
| dvě úsečky | `angle` |
| kružnice | `diameter` |
| oblouk | `radius` |

## Implementation status

What the app (`packages/app`) does differently from the tables above, or beyond them:

- **Přichycení a odvozené vazby**: přichycení na bod a na křivku je hotové; z odvozených vazeb jen vodorovná a svislá u volného konce úsečky.
- **Kóta mezi dvěma body** je vždy `distance`; vodorovná a svislá mají vlastní tlačítko, podle umístění myší se nerozlišují.
- **Vazby se zobrazují v seznamu** v panelu skici (s hodnotou, přepínačem řídicí/referenční a zvýrazněním konfliktních); v pohledu jsou jen popisky kót.
- **Vazby z katalogu (D34)**: tlačítka geometrických vazeb v panelu skici se berou z katalogu enginu včetně jména a nápovědy. Souměrnost: vyberte dva body a osu; střed úsečky: bod a úsečku. Tlačítko je dostupné, jen když výběr projde i vlastním pravidlem vazby (souměrný bod nesmí být koncem osy).
- **Equal radius and fixed point (D35, point 6)**: both are buttons from the catalog. *Equal radius* takes two circles or arcs. *Fixed point* takes one or more own points and fixes each where it is, with the unrounded position (a rounded coordinate could contradict what already holds the point); the origin and points of projected geometry are not offered. The row of a fixed point in the list of constraints has the fields *X* and *Y*: each takes a number or an expression, shows the value of an expression in its tooltip and is sent by `setField`. A constraint with named values other than a dimension gets such fields from the catalog, so the next one needs no change of the app.
- **Polygon (D35, point 7)**: the tool *Polygon* on the sketch bar takes two clicks, the centre and a vertex; both snap like the ends of any curve. While it is active the panel has its options: the number of sides (3 to 64, a hexagon by default) and the dimension of the size (none, the diameter of the circumscribed circle, or the size across flats, which is offered for an even number of sides only; with an odd number the choice falls back to the circle, as the select shows). The rubber band shows the circle and the polygon as it will be drawn. The dimension takes the size as drawn (rounded to 0.01 unless the vertex snapped to a point or a curve) and is then edited in the list of constraints like any other. The polygon is drawn turned as it should be and a constraint on a side then holds it: a constraint that has to turn it by more than about 30° can leave it folded (the sketch then has no closed region; *Zpět* brings the polygon back), see architecture.md, Sketch tools. The rectangle and the polygon are drawn by the sketch tools of the engine, the same ones MCP runs, and the snapping of all drawing tools uses the engine's `placeAnchor` and `anchorPosition`.
- **Fillet (D52)**: the tool *Fillet* on the sketch bar takes one click on a point where two lines meet; a click on no point says what the tool wants. While it is active the panel has its option *Radius*, a number or an expression (2 mm at first, remembered for the next corner; a radius that is not positive or an expression in error is marked and not taken). A refusal of the tool is said in the status line with its reason (the corner that is not one, the bound of a radius that does not fit). After a fillet the status line says how many features got the arc in their profile and names the features of `moved` and `shortened`. The batch is sent as the tool made it: the switch *Konstrukčně* does not turn its arc into a construction curve (the arc is one when its lines are). The context of the tool is made with the open sketch (`sketchContextOf`): the sketch, its solved state, the values of the parameters and the document; the rectangle and the polygon take the same context.
- **Re-point (D52)**: when the selection is one own curve and one own point or the origin that is not a point of the curve already, the panel of the sketch has a row *Point of* the curve (by its ID, *Point of c1*) with a choice of the points of the curve by its kind (*Start*, *End* of a line; *Centre* of a circle; *Centre*, *Start*, *End* of an arc) and the button *Re-point*, which sends `setCurvePoint`. The point of the curve nearest to the selected point is chosen at first; which one that is comes from the engine (`nearestCurvePoint`).
- **Trim, extend and split (D65)**: three tools of the sketch bar after *Fillet*, a file each in `packages/app/src/sketch-tools/` (`trim.ts`, `extend.ts`, `split.ts`, with what they share in `cut.ts`). Each picks curves (`picks: 'curve'`, so a click near a crossing still gives the curve, not the point there) and takes own curves only (`eligible`; *Extend* lines and arcs); which own curve a click means is asked of the engine (`curveUnder`): the curve the picker took, unless a construction line lying over a piece took the click from it. *Trim*: a click removes the piece of the curve under it between the neighbouring cuts, which is drawn dashed while the pointer is over it (`trimPiece`; nothing where the tool would refuse); a click away from every curve says what the tool wants. *Extend*: a click moves the end of the line or arc nearest to the click to the next curve its extension meets, drawn dashed while the pointer is over the curve (`extendEnd`, `extendReach`). *Split*: a click cuts the curve where other curves cross it. **Their panels**: *Up to* (all three) shows the curve the tool is bounded by (`with`), *Use the selected curve* takes the one curve selected and *Clear* drops it; the choice is kept with the sketch it was made in (the axes of the origin are in every sketch), so another sketch shows *the nearest curves* again. *Cut the other crossings of the outline first* (trim and extend) is the repair: the checkbox shows the default of the document (on when a feature reads regions of the sketch) until it is changed, and a change is kept with the sketch it was made in, so another sketch shows its own default again. *Split* has the button *Split crossings* (every contact of the outline at once, `split` without a curve) and the checkbox *Only at the crossing nearest to the click* (`at`). The batch is sent as the engine's tool made it (the switch *Konstrukčně* does not apply), and the status line names what followed and the features of `dropped`, `pending`, `reshaped`, `moved`, `shortened` and `broken` and the curves left whole (`uncut`); a refusal is said with its reason. **While the profile of the open sketch is invalid**, whatever the tool, its contacts are marked in the view (red points, from the query `sketchContacts`), and the panel under *Profil je neplatný* says how many there are and that *Split crossings* cuts them, or gives the query's reason when none can be cut. The hints of the buttons are fixed texts: they say what a click does.
- **The query of the contacts (D65)** has a view in the panel of queries (`QUERY_VIEWS`, `queries/sketch-contacts.ts`): a row per contact (its kind, the two curves, what *Split crossings* does there) and the marks in the view.
- **The tools of the sketch bar as a table (D64, part 4)**: a tool of the bar is a file in `packages/app/src/sketch-tools/` and one line of `SKETCH_TOOL_UI` (`index.ts`); the bar shows the tools in the order of the table, and the hint of the status line is the tool's `hint`. An entry says what the pointer picks (`picks`: a place in the sketch, points first, the default; a place on a curve only, of the curves `eligible` lets through, which a click near a crossing still gives; an edge of the model, *Promítnout*; or a picker of the tool's own), how many places a shape takes (`clicks`; without it every click goes to the tool, and the mode redraws the scene when places were kept before the click or are kept after it), what a click does with them (`click`, which returns the places to keep, so a line goes on from its end), what Enter does with the places kept (`finish`, no tool of today; called only when places are kept, it returns the places to keep, so too few for a shape stay; Enter the tool takes does not also press the button in focus), what it draws while the pointer moves (`draw`, the dashed shape in progress), whether it infers a horizontal or vertical line (`infer`), highlights what is under the pointer (`hovers`), drags points (`drags`) or takes the place under the click rather than the cursor of the last move (`atClick`: *Výběr* and *Fillet*), and its options with their part of the panel (`options`: the sides of the polygon, the radius of the fillet), which the app keeps by tool (`SketchState.tools`). The mode (`sketch-mode.ts`) keeps what all tools share. A drawing tool sends its batch through `drawn`, which applies the switch *Konstrukčně*; the fillet runs its batch as the engine made it. The tools of the app and the engine's `SKETCH_TOOLS` stay two catalogs: a tool here that runs an engine tool calls `sketchTool`.
- **Mirror and pattern (D68)**: three tools of the sketch bar after *Split* (`packages/app/src/sketch-tools/pattern.ts`): *Mirror* and *Linear pattern* pick a line that is not a copy (`picks: 'curve'`, an axis of the origin is one), *Circular pattern* a point that is not a copy. Each takes the own curves and points selected before it (and `origin`) as its sources, since choosing a tool keeps the selection; with none the status line says "Select the curves and points to copy first, then pick …" and nothing is sent. A click sends `addExternal` with a fresh ID (`m1` for a mirror, `p1` for a pattern), clears the selection and opens the form of the entry. While a pattern tool is active its panel has the count (3 at first) and the spacing (10 mm) or the angle (360°, which is the full turn; another angle is the angle from the source to the last copy), each a number or an expression, remembered by tool; an angle whose value is ±360° and that names no parameter (`360`, `2 * pi`, `deg(360)`) is the full turn (`fullTurn`, reading `expressionValue` of the engine); an angle that names a parameter is sent as an angle with its expression, so the pattern follows the parameter (at ±360° its last copy lies on the source and forms no regions). **The row** of an entry in the list of entries (headed *Projections, mirrors and patterns*) says *Mirror*, *Linear pattern*, *Grid* or *Circular pattern*, its ID, the number of sources and the axis, direction or centre with the counts and spans, "no copies" when the count makes none, "n open ends" when the result lists open ends of its copies (the first, with its distance, as the title of the row), and its warning as the rows of projections do; the hover marks its copies, and the row is marked (`selected`) while a copy of it is selected; ↻ picks the axis, direction or centre again in the view with the tool of its transform (`repair` of `REFERENCE_UI`, a function of the entry); ✕ removes it. A click on its name opens or closes **its form**, which is data (`form` of `REFERENCE_UI`: controls by the path of their field in the input form): the sources with *Use selection*, the kind of transform (a new kind keeps the line or the point where it fits), the axis, direction or centre as a select of the lines or points that may serve (not copies; the axes of the origin after the lines), the count and the span as fields that take an expression, the kind of span, and the switch *Second direction* (a grid). A change sends `updateExternal` with the whole input form, so the expressions of the other fields stay; a refusal is said with the engine's message. **The copies** are drawn dashed in the colour of projections, the copies of construction curves thinner, and their points have no handles (drawn only when selected or hovered); outside the sketch mode they are drawn as the curves they copy, a copy of a construction curve dashed (`CurveView.construction` of a copy is the flag of its source, through `derivedOrigin`). No drawing tool snaps to a copy (`anchorAt` skips them for every tool but *Výběr*); *Výběr* selects a copy, and a selection that holds a copy gets no constraint or dimension offer (`holdsCopy` of `sketch-commands.ts`). Delete on a selection that holds a copy deletes the own entities of the selection and says that the copy is not deleted by itself (its entry is removed by ✕ of its row, or its sources are changed in its form). After a trim, extend, split or fillet the status line names the mirrors and patterns the batch changed (`ids.entries`). The readers of the entities of the entries (`entryEntities`, `entryOf`, `entryTag`, `hoverMarks`, `lineIds`, the row) read the index of the engine (`entityIndex`, built once per sketch object), and a picker lists at most the first 100 entities of one derived entry (`PICKER_LIMIT`): the rest cannot be chosen in the app (a known limit; through MCP any copy is named by its ID), the picker says how many of the kind it lists it left out (a disabled option "… n more copies of `p1`, not listed"), and a value that names a copy it does not list is shown as a copy (`unlistedLabel`), not as missing, and a direction on such a point offers no repair.
- **The axes of the origin (D71)**: an open sketch shows `origin/x` and `origin/y` as two thin lines through its origin in the colour of a plane, a bucket of their own (`SceneModel.axes`, filled by `sketchScene`); the view draws each as its stretch inside what it shows, made again whenever the camera moves (`Viewport.placeAxes`, `stretchInView` of `draw.ts`), so an axis neither ends inside the view after a zoom far out nor is cut by the depth of the camera, and the fit of the camera does not read them. They are curves of the view of the sketch that are not own (`sketchView` reads `solvedCurves` of the engine), held by `origin` and shaped as whole lines (`lineExtent`): they are hovered (marked as any curve), selected and snapped to anywhere along them, a click near one adding a point held on it by `pointOnCurve`. A constraint is offered only when the selection has an own operand (the engine refuses one between fixed entities alone), and *Vodorovně* and *Svisle* go to the own lines of the selection only, so a line and an axis with *Vodorovně* make the line horizontal. A point and an axis offer *Bod na křivce* and the distance of the point to the axis, two points and an axis *Souměrně*, a line and an axis *Rovnoběžně*, *Kolmo* and the angle; *Stejná délka* and *Střed úsečky* are not offered with an axis and one axis alone has no length dimension (`hasLength` of `sketch-commands.ts`). Deleting an axis by the selection does nothing (the status line says what cannot be deleted). The label of a dimension to an axis stands beside what it measures, half way to the foot on the axis (`anchorOf`). The pickers of a line of a sketch list `origin/x` and `origin/y` after its lines (`lineIds`), labelled *origin/x (X axis of the sketch)* and *origin/y (Y axis of the sketch)* (`lineLabel`), so *Along a line of a sketch* of the axis picker is offered for every sketch and starts at `origin/x` of a sketch without lines.
- **Projection from another sketch (D69)**: the tool *From sketch* after *Promítnout* (`packages/app/src/sketch-tools/project-sketch.ts`) has its own picker (`picks: { pick }`): its candidates are the answers of the query `sketchSources`, asked once per shown state of the open sketch (the earlier sketches, then for each on a parallel plane every entity it lists), the latest sketch first; while the tool is active their curves are drawn dashed where a projection would put them. The picker takes the point under the pointer within the reach of a pick, else the nearest curve (`candidateAt`: points before curves, the first listed of equally near, so the later sketch where several lie on each other), and makes a place with a foreign snap. A click sends `addExternal` with `kind` from the candidate and `regions` from the option *Forms regions* (never for a point); with one projection from a sketch selected or one being repaired (↻ of its row), `updateExternal` keeping the ID, a refusal said with the engine's text (another kind, a duplicate). With *Whole sketch* a click runs the engine's tool `projectSketch` with the sketch under the pointer and the role of *As* (*as in its source*, *reference only*, *forming regions*, *own curves over them*), and the status line says how many entities and own curves it made and how many it left out. *From* limits the picker to one sketch; a sketch on a tilted plane is listed with its angle and disabled, one that did not evaluate likewise. **The row** of such an entry says *Point*, *Line*, *Circle* or *Arc*, its ID, the name of the source sketch and the entity (`Source: l`), the tag *projected*, ↻ by *From sketch*, and a checkbox *regions* for a curve (`flag` of `REFERENCE_UI`), which sends `updateExternal` with the flag switched. **In the model view** the curves of an entry whose row says `inModel` (a projection from a sketch, not a projected edge, whose edge lies there) are drawn with the own curves of their sketch, solid when they form regions and dashed otherwise, so a sketch of projections alone is seen outside the sketch mode. The query has a view (`queries/sketch-sources.ts`): a row per earlier sketch with its plane and counts, a row per entity with `from`, a picked one marked.
- **The entries of `external` (D64, part 5)**: the view draws an entry from the result of the sketch (its curves in `SketchResult.curves`, its points with a position) and the entities of the entry (`referenceEntities`), not from its fields; the pickers of an axis, a frame and the operands of a generator list the entities of the entries by kind, tagged by the word of their kind (*projected*). The row of an entry in the panel reads the table `REFERENCE_UI` by kind (`packages/app/src/references/`: the title, *Úsečka* or *Kružnice* for an edge, the detail, the body, and the button that repairs the entry by the tool *Promítnout*); it says *no geometry* when no entity of the entry has a position, and hovering it marks the curves of the entry. An entry is deleted by the selection of its own ID, as before, or by its row.
- **Konstrukční kreslení**: přepínač Konstrukčně na liště skici. Dokud je zapnutý, kreslicí nástroje zakládají konstrukční křivky (čárkovaně, netvoří profil); odvozené vazby vznikají stejně. Existující křivky se přepínají tlačítkem v panelu jako dřív.
- **Diagnostika skici (D32)** je v panelu pod stavem skici:
  - Souhrn „Vazby navíc: N, z toho duplicity: M". V seznamu jsou označené právě vazby ze sady, kterou engine navrhuje odebrat (`navíc`, `duplicita`); starší vazby ze stejné závislé skupiny označené nejsou. Tlačítko maže jen duplicity, u ostatních volí uživatel.
  - U skici v konfliktu se ze souhrnu ukáže jen počet duplicit a tlačítko pro jejich smazání (smazání může konflikt odstranit).
  - Kliknutí na řádek vazby ji označí. Pod řádkem se pak jednou větou řekne, jestli je potřebná, nebo ze kterých vazeb plyne; ty se v seznamu podbarví a v pohledu se zvýrazní entity označené vazby (azurově) a vazeb, ze kterých plyne (fialově). Druhé kliknutí nebo Esc označení ruší.
  - Volné entity jsou v pohledu modré, určené tmavé. Konstrukční křivky si nechávají svou barvu a čárkování; volnost ukazují jejich body. Dokud skica není vyřešená, o volnosti se nedá nic říct a vlastní geometrie je šedomodrá. Panel volné body a poloměry vyjmenuje, umí je vybrat a poradí, když skicu dourčí ukotvení jednoho bodu.
  - Vazbu, kterou skica už má, aplikace nepřidá a řekne to; ze sady vazeb přidá jen ty, které chybí. Platí to i pro referenční kótu mezi pevnými entitami.
